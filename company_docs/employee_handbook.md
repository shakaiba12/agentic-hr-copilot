## 1. Welcome & Company Culture
Welcome to the team! Our mission is to empower workforce intelligence through transparency, empathy, and technical excellence.

---

## 2. Employment Terms & Probation Period
- **Probationary Period**: All new hires undergo a standard **90-calendar-day probationary period**. Performance is formally reviewed at 45 and 90 days.
- **Notice Period**: Employees are requested to give at least **two (2) weeks (14 calendar days)** written notice of resignation. Management and executive positions require **four (4) weeks (30 calendar days)** notice.

---

## 3. Performance Review Cycles
- **Cadence**: Formal comprehensive performance reviews occur annually in **Q4 (November/December)**, with mid-year check-ins in **Q2 (June)**.
- **Rating Scale**: 1.0 (Needs Improvement) to 5.0 (Exceptional Performance). A rating of 3.0 represents 'Meets High Standards'.
- **Eligibility for Promotion**: Employees must maintain a performance rating of **4.0 or above** for two consecutive cycles and have at least 12 months in their current role.

---

## 4. Code of Conduct & Workplace Harassment
We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or unethical behavior. Reports can be filed anonymously via the People Operations portal.
