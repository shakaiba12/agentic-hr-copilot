# Global HR Policy — Compensation and Benefits

**Document ID**: POL-HR-2026-03  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Version**: 3.0  
**Owner**: Total Rewards & People Operations  

---

## 1. Salary Structure & Annual Merit Increases
- Base salary is reviewed annually following the Q4 performance appraisal cycle.
- Merit increases range from 2.0% to 8.0% depending on individual performance rating and market compa-ratio.

---

## 2. Retirement & 401(k) Matching
- **Eligibility**: Full-time employees after completing **six (6) months of continuous service**.
- **Company Match**: 50% match on employee contributions up to a maximum of **6% of base annual salary** (maximum employer contribution of 3%).
- **Vesting**: 100% immediate vesting on company matching funds from day one of enrollment.

---

## 3. Health, Dental & Vision Insurance
- **Coverage**: 100% premium coverage for full-time employees, and 70% coverage for eligible dependents.
- **Effective Date**: First day of the calendar month following the date of hire.

---

## 4. Continuing Education & Tuition Assistance
- **Eligibility**: Full-time employees with at least **one (1) year (12 months) of continuous service** in good performance standing (rating >= 3.0).
- **Annual Allowance**: Up to **$3,000 per calendar year** for accredited university courses, technical certifications, and job-related training programs.
- **Repayment Agreement**: Requires employee to remain with the company for 12 months following reimbursement.
