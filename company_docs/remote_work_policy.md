# Global HR Policy — Remote and Hybrid Work

**Document ID**: POL-HR-2026-02  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Version**: 2.1  
**Owner**: People Operations  

---

## 1. Scope and Eligibility
- All full-time employees who have completed their **ninety (90) day probationary period** are eligible to apply for remote or hybrid work arrangements.
- Departments eligible for standard hybrid work (up to 2 remote days per week): Engineering, Product, Marketing, People Operations, and Finance.
- Roles requiring in-person operations (Facilities, On-site IT support) must maintain a minimum of 4 on-site days per week.

---

## 2. Remote Work Models
1. **Hybrid Model**: 3 days in office, 2 days remote per week (Core office days: Tuesday and Thursday).
2. **Fully Remote Model**: Permitted upon VP-level approval for individual contributors in Engineering and Research.

---

## 3. Core Working Hours and Availability
- All employees, regardless of location, must be available and responsive during **Core Collaboration Hours**: **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** in their designated home office time zone.
- Team standups and department all-hands meetings are mandatory attendance.

---

## 4. Equipment and Home Office Stipend
- **One-time Stipend**: Eligible remote/hybrid employees receive a one-time home office setup stipend of **$500** for ergonomic chairs, monitors, or desks.
- **Company Hardware**: Standard company laptop and security hardware are provided and maintained by the IT Department.
