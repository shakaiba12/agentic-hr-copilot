# Global HR Policy — Employee Leave and Time Off

**Document ID**: POL-HR-2026-01  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Version**: 3.2  
**Owner**: People Operations  

---

## 1. Overview and Scope
This policy outlines the leave benefits and eligibility criteria for all full-time and part-time employees across all global offices.

---

## 2. Annual Vacation Leave
- **Accrual**: Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year (1.67 days per month).
- **Rollover**: A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may be rolled over into the following calendar year and must be used by March 31.
- **Notice**: Vacation requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days require manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance.

---

## 3. Sick and Wellness Leave
- **Allowance**: All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, available from their first day of employment.
- **Medical Certification**: Absences of 3 or more consecutive business days require a doctor's note or medical certificate submitted to People Operations.
- **Mental Health Days**: Up to 2 sick leave days per year can be designated as mental wellness days without medical documentation.

---

## 4. Parental and Family Leave

### 4.1 Maternity Leave
- **Eligibility**: Female employees with at least **twelve (12) consecutive months of service** prior to the expected delivery date are eligible for full maternity leave benefits.
- **Duration**: Eligible employees receive **16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave**.
- **Extension**: An additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave may be requested subject to department manager and People Operations approval.
- **Health Coverage**: Full medical, dental, and vision insurance benefits remain active during the entire leave period.

### 4.2 Paternity and Partner Leave
- **Eligibility**: Non-birthing parents and adoptive parents with at least **six (6) months of service** prior to birth or adoption.
- **Duration**: **4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave**, which may be taken continuously or in two 2-week blocks within the first 12 months following birth or adoption.

---

## 5. Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 consecutive business days of paid leave.
- Extended family member (grandparent, aunt/uncle, in-laws): 3 consecutive business days of paid leave.

---

## 6. Sabbatical Leave
- **Eligibility**: Employees with **five (5) or more years of continuous full-time service** in good standing (performance rating >= 3.5).
- **Duration**: Up to 3 months of unpaid sabbatical with job protection and continuation of health benefits.
